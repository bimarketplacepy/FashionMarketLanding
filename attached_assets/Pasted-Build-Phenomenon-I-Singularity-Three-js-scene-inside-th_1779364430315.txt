Build Phenomenon I (Singularity) Three.js scene inside the existing 
section 1 placeholder. Do not touch any other section.

CONCEPT

A spinning black hole with a glowing accretion disk and starfield 
background. Camera dollies in as the user scrolls through this section, 
making the black hole loom larger. Visually inspired by Interstellar 
and Event Horizon Telescope imagery.

CANVAS SETUP

Mount a Three.js scene inside the existing canvas placeholder for 
section 1 (the canvas-singularity div). Scene must:

- Full viewport size, resize on window resize
- WebGL2 renderer with antialias true, alpha false (black bg)
- Pixel ratio capped at 2 (Math.min(window.devicePixelRatio, 2))
- Output encoding sRGB
- Pause the render loop when section is outside the viewport (use 
  IntersectionObserver on the section element)

SCENE COMPOSITION

1. STARFIELD BACKGROUND
- 8000 stars as Points with custom shader
- Distributed on the surface of a large sphere (radius 200) using 
  spherical random distribution
- Each star has slight size variation (0.5 to 2.0 pixels)
- Each star has slight colour variation (warm white #fffaf0 to cool 
  blue-white #e8f0ff)
- Subtle twinkling: brightness oscillates with sine wave per star, 
  random phase
- Additive blending

2. EVENT HORIZON (the black sphere)
- SphereGeometry, radius 1.0
- MeshBasicMaterial with colour pure black #000000
- This is the inescapable centre

3. PHOTON RING (the glowing edge)
- TorusGeometry just slightly larger than the event horizon 
  (radius 1.05, tube 0.02)
- MeshBasicMaterial with emissive bright orange #ffb877
- Tilted slightly off the camera axis
- Subtle pulse: scale oscillates 1.0 to 1.03 over 4 seconds

4. ACCRETION DISK (the spectacle)

This is the centrepiece. Build it from 50,000 particles:

- BufferGeometry with 50,000 positions
- Particles distributed in a flat disk (Y near zero) between radius 1.4 
  and radius 5.0
- Disk thickness on Y axis: very thin, around 0.05 units, with noise
- Each particle stores:
  - Initial radius (for orbital speed calculation)
  - Initial angle
  - Vertical offset within the thin disk
- Custom ShaderMaterial:
  - Vertex shader: animates orbital position based on time and initial 
    radius (Keplerian rotation: inner particles orbit faster, omega 
    proportional to 1/sqrt(radius))
  - Fragment shader: colours each particle based on radius
    - Inner band (radius 1.4 to 1.8): white-hot #fff8e1
    - Mid band (radius 1.8 to 3.0): hot orange #ffa830
    - Outer band (radius 3.0 to 5.0): deep red-orange #d04420
    - Edge fade: alpha falls off from radius 4.5 to 5.0
  - Additive blending
  - Depth write false
- Particle size: 1.5 pixels with slight per-particle variation
- Disk tilted approximately 18 degrees off horizontal so the camera 
  sees the disk edge-on-ish with some bend

5. INNER GLOW HALO
- A small additional point-based glow concentrated right at the inner 
  edge of the accretion disk to suggest the hottest plasma
- 5000 particles at radius 1.4 to 1.7, hottest white #ffffff with 
  yellow tinge, additive blend, soft circular sprite

CAMERA

PerspectiveCamera, FOV 50.
Initial position: x=0, y=2.5, z=10 (looking slightly down at the disk)
LookAt: origin (0, 0, 0)

SCROLL-DRIVEN CAMERA

As the user scrolls through section 1:
- 0% progress: camera z=10
- 100% progress: camera z=4.2
- Y position: 2.5 at 0%, decreasing to 0.8 at 100% (sweeping down toward 
  disk plane)
- Use GSAP ScrollTrigger pinned to section 1, scrubbing camera position 
  with progress

Animation continues whether scrolled or not (disk rotates, photon ring 
pulses, stars twinkle). The scroll only drives camera position.

POST-PROCESSING

EffectComposer with:
- RenderPass
- UnrealBloomPass:
  - threshold: 0.7
  - strength: 0.55
  - radius: 0.45
- OutputPass

Bloom must be subtle. If the disk blows out and loses definition, 
reduce strength to 0.45. Aim for glow, not haze.

PERFORMANCE

- Renderer paused when section is fully out of viewport
- Limit DPR to 2
- Use BufferGeometry, not Geometry
- Single material instance per group

DO NOT

- Do not add OrbitControls. The user scroll drives the camera, not the 
  mouse.
- Do not add bloom strength above 0.6.
- Do not add UnrealBloomPass without OutputPass after it (causes 
  colour space issues).
- Do not change anything outside section 1.
