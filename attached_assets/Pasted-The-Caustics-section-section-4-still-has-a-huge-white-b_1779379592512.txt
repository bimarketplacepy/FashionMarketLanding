The Caustics section (section 4) still has a huge white bloom wash 
dominating the middle and lower portion of the frame. The previous 
bloom rebalance reduced UnrealBloomPass strength but the underlying 
bright sources are still too hot, so even modest bloom amplifies them 
into a featureless wash.

Apply these aggressive fixes to Caustics ONLY. Do not touch any other 
section.

FIX 1: REDUCE BLOOM FURTHER ON CAUSTICS

- UnrealBloomPass threshold: 0.92 (was 0.88)
- UnrealBloomPass strength: 0.12 (was 0.22)
- UnrealBloomPass radius: 0.3 (was 0.4)

Bloom is now near-off. The scene must hold visual interest on its own 
without leaning on bloom.

FIX 2: REDUCE GOD RAYS BRIGHTNESS

The god ray cylinders are likely the main bloom source. In the god 
ray shader:
- Reduce the soft cyan-white emissive colour alpha to 0.06 (was 0.15 
  at top tapering to 0.02 at bottom)
- Or equivalently: multiply the final fragment colour by 0.4 in the 
  shader
- This dims the god rays to a subtle suggestion rather than dominant 
  beams

FIX 3: REDUCE CAUSTIC COLOUR INTENSITY

In the seabed fragment shader, the composite line:
finalColor = baseColor + causticColor * finalCaustic * 1.8

Change the multiplier from 1.8 to 0.9. The caustic network should be 
visible as bright cyan lines but not approaching pure white.

FIX 4: REMOVE THE SEABED CENTRE EMISSIVE (THE BLOOM-BOMB SOURCE)

The auditor identified a specific bloom-bomb source at the 
bottom-centre of the seabed. Search for:
- Any PointLight, DirectionalLight, or SpotLight positioned at or 
  near the bottom-centre of the seabed (around 0, -5, 0)
- Any sun/sunlight emissive plane or sphere positioned at the seabed
- Any caustic peak emissive multiplier specifically at the centre

If any of these exist with high intensity, either:
- Remove the source entirely
- Or cut its intensity to 25% of current value

FIX 5: DARKEN THE BASE SEABED COLOUR

Currently base seabed colour is #1a4f5a. Darken to #102e36 so the 
overall scene is darker and the caustic web lines stand out more 
clearly against deeper shadow. This also gives less material for 
bloom to wash out.

FIX 6: VERIFY WATER SURFACE BRIGHTNESS

The water surface (the plane above the camera showing the underside 
of the water) may be too bright. In its shader:
- Reduce the base silver-cyan colour from #8be0f0 to #5a96a8 (darker, 
  less saturated)
- Reduce the highlight colour intensity by 40%

EXPECTED RESULT

The lower portion of the frame should now show:
- A darker seabed with clearly visible thin cyan caustic line network 
  across the entire visible area (not just the edges)
- Subtle god rays descending into the scene without dominating
- No central white wash
- Water surface above showing rippling silver shimmer at moderate 
  brightness

If after this the scene feels too dark, raise the seabed base colour 
slightly to #1a3e48 (between current and the original) but do NOT 
increase bloom or god ray brightness.
