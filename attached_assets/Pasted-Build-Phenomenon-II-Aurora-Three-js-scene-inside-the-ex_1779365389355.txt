Build Phenomenon II (Aurora) Three.js scene inside the existing 
section 2 placeholder. Do not touch any other section.

CONCEPT

The Northern Lights as flowing vertical ribbons of light. Twelve aurora 
curtains hang from the upper sky and dance, distorted by curl noise. 
Camera slowly rises and pushes forward as the user scrolls, making the 
ribbons loom overhead. Starfield background. No ground.

CANVAS SETUP

Mount a Three.js scene inside the existing canvas placeholder for 
section 2 (canvas-aurora div). Same renderer setup as Singularity:
- WebGL2, antialias true, alpha false
- Pixel ratio capped at 2
- Output encoding sRGB
- Pause RAF when section is out of viewport (IntersectionObserver)

SCENE COMPOSITION

1. STARFIELD BACKGROUND
- 6000 stars on a sphere at radius 200, same shader style as Singularity 
  but darker overall (max brightness 0.6 not 1.0)
- Cool blue-white tint #d8e8ff

2. AURORA RIBBONS (the centrepiece)

Build twelve flowing ribbon curtains:

- For each ribbon: PlaneGeometry with width 4, height 14, 32 width 
  segments, 128 height segments
- Each ribbon positioned at:
  - X position: random distribution across -16 to +16
  - Y position: centred at y=4 (so ribbon spans from y=-3 to y=11)
  - Z position: random distribution across -8 to +2 (depth variation)
- Each ribbon rotated to face roughly toward camera with slight random 
  yaw (-15 to +15 degrees)
- Each ribbon has a unique seed value for noise offset

Custom ShaderMaterial per ribbon (or single material with per-instance 
attributes, your choice):

VERTEX SHADER behaviour:
- Take the original vertex position
- Apply curl noise displacement on X and Z axes based on:
  - The vertex's Y position (more displacement at top, less at bottom)
  - Time (continuous flowing motion, around 0.3 units per second)
  - The ribbon's unique seed
- Use classic simplex 3D noise (the Ashima webgl-noise implementation 
  is fine) sampled at scale around 0.4 for large flowing waves plus a 
  second octave at scale 1.6 for finer ripples
- Bend the ribbon top slightly outward in random direction for natural 
  feel
- Maximum horizontal displacement at top: around 2.5 units
- The bottom edge (y=-3 local) barely moves (0.2 units max)

FRAGMENT SHADER behaviour:
- Compute vertical position normalised (0 at bottom, 1 at top of ribbon)
- Compute horizontal position normalised (0 to 1 across width)
- Colour gradient by vertical position:
  - Bottom (0 to 0.15): pure black, full transparency
  - Lower (0.15 to 0.4): emissive green #1a8c4e fading in
  - Mid (0.4 to 0.7): bright teal-green #22d4a8 (peak brightness here)
  - Upper (0.7 to 0.92): violet-pink #b478ff
  - Top (0.92 to 1.0): deep violet #5a3aff fading to transparent
- Edge fade: alpha drops off near horizontal edges (0 to 0.1 and 0.9 
  to 1.0) for soft curtain edges
- Brightness modulation: sample a low-frequency noise based on vertical 
  position and time to create bright bands flowing down the ribbon. 
  Bands move downward at around 0.4 units per second.
- Additive blending
- Depth write false
- Transparent true

3. SOFT VOLUMETRIC GLOW
- Behind the ribbons, place a few large soft glowing sprites 
  (PlaneGeometry 20x20, additive blend) tinted teal and violet, 
  positioned at random Z depths between -10 and -4, very low opacity 
  (alpha 0.1), slowly drifting horizontally
- This adds atmospheric haze behind the curtains

CAMERA

PerspectiveCamera, FOV 60.
Initial position: x=0, y=0, z=12 (looking forward at the curtains)
LookAt: x=0, y=4, z=0 (looking slightly up at the curtain centres)

SCROLL-DRIVEN CAMERA

As scroll progresses through section 2:
- 0% progress: position z=12, y=0
- 100% progress: position z=2, y=3 (camera rises and pushes into the 
  curtains, looking up at them dramatically)
- LookAt y-component shifts from 4 to 7 over the scroll (so you look 
  up more as you get close)
- Use GSAP ScrollTrigger pinned to section 2, scrubbing camera 
  position and look target with progress

The ribbon animation continues whether scrolled or not.

POST-PROCESSING

EffectComposer with:
- RenderPass
- UnrealBloomPass:
  - threshold: 0.5
  - strength: 0.45
  - radius: 0.55
- OutputPass

Bloom should produce a soft luminous glow on the ribbon highlights, not 
a hazy white blowout. If brightness gets crushed, reduce strength.

ATMOSPHERIC EXTRAS

- Subtle background fog: scene.fog = new THREE.FogExp2(0x000511, 0.02) 
  for distance fade-out
- Very faint vertical light shafts from the curtains using additional 
  cone-shaped meshes with soft additive shader is optional, skip if 
  performance suffers

PERFORMANCE TARGETS

- Should hit 60fps on M4 MacBook in Chrome
- Total particle/vertex count under 200,000
- Single draw call per ribbon (12 ribbons total)
- Stars in a single Points geometry

DO NOT

- Do not add OrbitControls
- Do not increase bloom strength above 0.5
- Do not change anything outside section 2
