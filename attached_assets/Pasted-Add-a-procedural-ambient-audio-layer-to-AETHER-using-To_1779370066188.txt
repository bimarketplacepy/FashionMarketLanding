Add a procedural ambient audio layer to AETHER using Tone.js. The audio 
builds across all sections, with each phenomena section having its own 
sonic character matching the visual. Single mute toggle, starts muted 
due to browser autoplay policy.

Do not change any visual Three.js code.

INSTALL AND SETUP

Install Tone.js if not already installed.

Create a single Tone.js audio context that initialises on first user 
interaction (button click). Before that, all synths exist but the 
master volume is at -Infinity.

MASTER CHAIN

Tone.Destination → master Tone.Volume node (starts at -Infinity dB, 
ramps to -12 dB on unmute) → master Tone.Reverb (decay 4 seconds, 
wet 0.4) → master Tone.Limiter (-1 dB threshold for safety)

PER-SECTION SYNTHS

Build seven layered voices. Each has its own gain node feeding into 
the master chain. Each gain is scroll-driven: fades up as you enter 
its section and fades down as you leave.

VOICE 1: DEEP DRONE (active across the whole site, baseline)
- Tone.AMSynth with low frequency
- Notes: D1 and A1 (open fifth) layered
- Slow attack (4s), no release (sustained)
- Always at -24 dB regardless of section

VOICE 2: SINGULARITY (section 1)
- Tone.FMSynth, ominous low register
- Base note: G1, with a second voice at C2
- Filter: Tone.AutoFilter at 0.1 Hz, slow sweep
- Distortion: Tone.Distortion 0.15 for grit
- Fades in -infinity to -10 dB across section 1
- Sustained chord

VOICE 3: AURORA (section 2)
- Tone.PolySynth (sine wave), high register pad
- Notes: D5, F#5, A5, C#6 (D major 7 voicing)
- Slow attack (3s), long release (5s)
- Tone.FeedbackDelay at 3/8 note, feedback 0.5, wet 0.4
- Shimmery, ethereal
- Fades in across section 2 to -14 dB

VOICE 4: CRYSTALLINE (section 3)
- Tone.MetalSynth (bell-like)
- Random pitches from D minor pentatonic, high octaves (D5, F5, G5, A5, C6)
- Triggered randomly every 1.5 to 3 seconds, one bell hit
- Each hit at -20 dB
- Only triggers when section 3 is in viewport
- Sparkling, intermittent

VOICE 5: CAUSTICS (section 4)
- Tone.MonoSynth with lowpass filter (cutoff 1500 Hz, Q 4)
- Notes: A2, then E3, alternating every 8 seconds
- Tone.Tremolo at 0.4 Hz, depth 0.6 (gives a watery wobble)
- Tone.PingPongDelay at 1/4 note, feedback 0.3, wet 0.5
- Fades in across section 4 to -12 dB
- Underwater, hypnotic

VOICE 6: PARTICLE STORM (section 5)
- Tone.NoiseSynth (pink noise) gated with Tone.AutoFilter (LFO 0.5 Hz)
- Plus a Tone.PluckSynth playing random notes from D dorian scale 
  (D, E, F, G, A, B, C) at irregular intervals (between 0.3 and 1.2 
  seconds apart)
- Pluck notes octave: D3 to D5 random
- Fades in across section 5 to -14 dB
- Granular, organic

VOICE 7: WORMHOLE (section 6)
- Tone.PolySynth (sawtooth wave), ascending arpeggios
- Loop through notes: D3, A3, D4, F4, A4, D5, F5, A5 (ascending pattern)
- Trigger one note every 1/8 note at tempo 110 BPM
- Tone.FeedbackDelay at 3/16 note, feedback 0.6, wet 0.6
- Filter envelope sweeping up (Tone.Filter cutoff modulated from 
  500 Hz to 5000 Hz over the section's duration based on scroll progress)
- Fades in across section 6 to -10 dB
- Tension building, climactic

VOICE 8: OUTRO (section 7) - resolution
- Tone.PolySynth (triangle wave), held chord
- D major chord: D4, F#4, A4, D5 sustained
- Very long attack (6s), gentle
- Wet reverb, almost ambient pad
- Fades in across section 7 to -16 dB
- Calm resolution

SCROLL-DRIVEN MIXING

Use GSAP ScrollTrigger to drive each voice's gain based on scroll 
position. Each voice has a fade-in window (the section before its 
section + first half of its section) and a fade-out window (second 
half of its section + first half of next section). Overlap creates 
crossfades between sonic worlds.

Voice 1 (Deep Drone): always at -24 dB once audio starts.

USER INTERFACE

A small mute toggle button in the bottom-right corner of the viewport:
- Fixed position, 32px from bottom, 32px from right
- 40px x 40px circular button
- Icon: speaker icon (muted state) or speaker with sound waves (unmuted)
- Style: transparent background, 1px off-white border, off-white icon, 
  hover changes background to off-white 10% opacity
- z-index: 10000 (always on top)

On first click: start the Tone.js audio context, ramp master volume 
from -Infinity to -12 dB over 1.5 seconds, change icon to unmuted state.

On subsequent clicks: toggle between muted (-Infinity dB) and unmuted 
(-12 dB), ramp over 400ms.

State persists in sessionStorage so the mute preference survives scroll 
position changes (but resets on tab close).

INITIAL STATE

Audio starts muted on page load. The mute button shows the muted icon. 
This prevents autoplay blocking.

A tiny tooltip appears the first time the user scrolls past section 0:
"Sound recommended" in JetBrains Mono 10px letter-spaced, off-white 60% 
opacity, positioned 20px above the mute button. Auto-dismisses after 
4 seconds or after the user clicks the mute button.

PERFORMANCE

- All synths share one Tone.context
- Reverb is one instance shared via send/return
- Audio runs continuously regardless of scroll position (no start/stop, 
  just gain modulation)

DO NOT

- Do not start the audio context automatically (browser blocks it)
- Do not play any synths above -10 dB
- Do not change any Three.js visual code
